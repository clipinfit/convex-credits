---
title: Convex Credits
description: Credits for background jobs.
---

Convex Credits stores account balances, charges, and credit movements inside your Convex deployment.

Reserve credits before a job starts. Complete the charge after success. Release it after failure. A completed job produces one debit.

This package is in development. See [release status](/docs/release-status) before adoption.

Start with [installation](/docs/getting-started), then read the [charge lifecycle](/docs/lifecycle) and [host contract](/docs/host-contract).
