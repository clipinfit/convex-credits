---
title: Charge lifecycle
description: One charge, explicit outcomes.
---

## Reserve

`reserve` deducts available credits and creates a pending charge. The movement is negative. Other jobs cannot spend those credits.

## Complete

`complete` changes a pending charge to completed. It creates no credit movement. Repeating completion is safe. Completing a released charge fails with `CHARGE_RELEASED`.

## Release

`release` returns all credits from a pending charge. It creates one positive movement linked to the charge. Repeating release cannot refund twice. Releasing a completed charge fails with `CHARGE_COMPLETED`.

## Refund

`refund` returns credits after completion. It accepts an amount and a reason. Partial refunds are supported. Total refunds cannot exceed the original charge.

## Balances and history

Available balance is the amount another job may reserve. History shows actual signed changes. Charge state records whether work is pending, completed, or released. These are separate concepts.

The component does not call your AI provider. Your workflow must decide when success or failure is final. Do not release a charge merely because a request timed out while its job may still complete.
